Google SSO logs a user in on email match alone

High CWE-287 GoogleAuthController

If someone signs in with Google and the email matches an existing account, they are let in without checking that Google actually verified that email. An attacker with an unverified Google address that happens to match a real user's email can take over the account.

app/Modules/Web/Auth/Controllers/GoogleAuthController.php:41
  $user = User::where('email', $google->email)->first();   // no email_verified check

Also affects: the Microsoft and Zendesk OAuth callbacks share this handler.
